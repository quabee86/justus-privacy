JUST-US Child Safety Standards

Standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Our Commitment

JUST-US has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). JUST-US is intended for adults and does not permit children or minors to use dating or matchmaking features. We prohibit any content, behavior, account, communication, or activity that sexually exploits, abuses, grooms, endangers, or traffics a child.

Prohibited Conduct

Users must not use JUST-US to:

  • Sexually exploit or abuse a child or minor.
  • Groom, target, solicit, manipulate, or attempt to meet a child for sexual purposes.
  • Create, upload, request, possess, share, distribute, or promote child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexualize or solicit sexual images, videos, or other sexual content involving minors.
  • Engage in sextortion, blackmail, threats, or coercion involving a child.
  • Advertise, arrange, facilitate, or promote the sexual trafficking or commercial exploitation of a child.
  • Misrepresent an adult's age or identity for the purpose of contacting or exploiting a minor.
  • Encourage another person to engage in any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Age Requirement

JUST-US is an adults-only dating service. Users must meet the minimum age requirement applicable to JUST-US and Google Play. Dating and matchmaking functionality is not intended for minors.

Attempting to bypass age restrictions or providing false age information may result in account suspension or permanent removal.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you believe a user, profile, message, image, video, or other activity on JUST-US may involve CSAE or CSAM, report it immediately using the reporting tools available inside the app or contact our child-safety team.

Please do not send, forward, or attach suspected CSAM unnecessarily. Provide enough information for us to identify the account or content and investigate the report safely.

Enforcement

We investigate reports and take appropriate action based on the available evidence, applicable law, and our policies. Enforcement may include removing content, restricting features, suspending accounts, permanently terminating accounts, preserving relevant information where legally appropriate, and cooperating with lawful requests from authorities.

Accounts suspected of facilitating CSAE or CSAM may be removed without prior notice where appropriate.

CSAM Handling

JUST-US prohibits CSAM and will take appropriate action when we obtain actual knowledge of suspected CSAM, consistent with applicable law and our published policies. We may remove or restrict access to prohibited material, preserve relevant information where legally permitted or required, and make reports to appropriate authorities when required by law or otherwise appropriate.

Child Safety Point of Contact

Our designated child-safety contact is responsible for receiving child-safety reports and responding to potential CSAE concerns, including notifications relating to content or behavior found on the JUST-US platform.

Cooperation With Authorities

JUST-US will cooperate with appropriate law-enforcement and child-protection authorities as required by applicable law. Where required, confirmed CSAM and other qualifying reports will be handled and reported to the appropriate regional authority.

User Safety and In-App Reporting

JUST-US provides users with mechanisms to report concerns and problematic behavior. Users should use the in-app report and block features whenever available and should not engage with a suspected offender.

We encourage users to preserve relevant account information when making a report, without downloading, copying, or redistributing illegal material.

Policy Updates

JUST-US may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time to improve safety, comply with applicable law, and respond to changes in platform requirements. The current version will be published on this page.
